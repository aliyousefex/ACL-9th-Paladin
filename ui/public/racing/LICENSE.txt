Images used by the onboard race preview on the home page
=========================================================

Every image in this folder is derived from assets published by Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com) under the Creative Commons
CC0 1.0 Universal public-domain dedication (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). CC0 requires no attribution;
it is given here because the authors deserve it.

  sky.jpg
      "Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky)" (kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky) by Greg Zaal, sky edits by Jarod Guest.
      https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky
      The 16k tone-mapped JPEG, reduced to 2048 x 1024.

  asphalt-diff.jpg, asphalt-nor.jpg, asphalt-rough.jpg
      "Asphalt Track" (asphalt_track) by Dimitrios Savva.
      https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_track
      The 1k diffuse, normal (OpenGL) and roughness maps, re-encoded as JPEG (roughness at 512 px, one channel).

  grass-diff.jpg, grass-nor.jpg, grass-rough.jpg
      "Aerial Grass Rock" (aerial_grass_rock) by Rob Tuytel.
      https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_grass_rock
      The 1k diffuse, normal (OpenGL) and roughness maps, re-encoded as JPEG (roughness at 512 px, one channel).

  tree-fir-a.webp, tree-fir-b.webp, tree-broad-a.webp, tree-broad-b.webp, bush-a.webp, bush-b.webp
      Rendered from the 1k glTF scans "Fir Sapling" (fir_sapling), "Fir Sapling Medium" (fir_sapling_medium),
      "Island Tree 01" (island_tree_01), "Island Tree 03" (island_tree_03) by Rob Tuytel and Rico Cilliers, and
      "Shrub 02" (shrub_02) and "Shrub 04" (shrub_04) by Rico Cilliers.
      https://polyhaven.com/a/fir_sapling  https://polyhaven.com/a/fir_sapling_medium  https://polyhaven.com/a/island_tree_01
      https://polyhaven.com/a/island_tree_03  https://polyhaven.com/a/shrub_02  https://polyhaven.com/a/shrub_04
      Each scan was lit in daylight and rendered once, from the side, on a transparent background. The scans themselves are not
      shipped: they are far too large for a web page.

  poster.jpg
      A frame of the onboard preview itself, drawn at the start of the race, shown while the 3D scene loads.

Only resizing, re-encoding and rendering were applied to the Poly Haven assets. Everything else the scene draws (the circuit, the barriers
and their adverts, the cars, the gloves and the grandstands) is made in code, and the cars' liveries are invented colour schemes, not
any team's.
