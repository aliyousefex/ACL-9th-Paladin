## Implemented but requiring further validation

- [ ] Distribution calibration by circuit subgroup; current held-out bins and year slices are global/era-level because circuit samples are small.
- [ ] Decision-impact ranking from forecast revisions; deterministic rules need expert review.

## In progress

- [ ] Forecast-change decomposition and multiple-moment comparison in Decision Replay.
- [ ] Visual image/card export for creator workflows.

## Blocked by concrete dependency

- [ ] Tyre compound and degradation modelling — absent from the course dataset.
- [ ] Weather-aware forecasting — no historical weather table in the course dataset.
- [ ] Safety-car/VSC cause modelling — race-control event stream unavailable.
- [ ] Wall-clock availability replay — source files retain lap numbers, not trustworthy publication timestamps.
- [ ] Commercial live feed — requires explicit data rights and credentials.

## Deferred for scientific or product reasons

- [ ] Reinforcement learning — no validated environment or logged action-policy data.
- [ ] Causal team-decision grades — unobserved team information makes authoritative labels indefensible.
- [ ] High-fidelity vehicle digital twin — course data cannot identify vehicle physics.
- [ ] Transformer/GNN models — must first beat simpler chronological baselines with available sample size.
- [ ] Backend/accounts/collaboration — no implemented workflow currently requires operational infrastructure.
